Ants Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Ants: Pixel Puzzle collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your iPhone

The game saves your progress and settings on your device: which pictures you've unlocked, your best stars, the picture you were playing, and your choices for speed, scoring, sound and vibration. This never leaves your iPhone, and nobody else can see it, including the developer. Deleting the app deletes it. If you use iCloud or device backups, it may be included in those backups like other app data, under Apple's own privacy terms.

Children

The game collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be asked through the project's GitHub issues.
