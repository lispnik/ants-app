Support

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page: github.com/lispnik/ants-app/issues. Please include your iPhone model, the iOS version, the app version (shown in the App Store), and the picture you were playing.

How to play

Tap the box at the front of a column to send it to a free slot. Its ants walk out, each one taking a cube of its own color that it can reach, and carry it to a hole. An empty box leaves its slot free. Clear every cube to win the picture; fill every slot with boxes whose cubes are all still buried, and the round is lost.

Common questions

There's no sound. The game's sounds respect Silent mode, so they mix with your music instead of stopping it. Turn Silent mode off, and check that Sound is on in the game's settings (the gear button left of the slots).

How do I turn off the vibration, or the timer and stars? Tap the gear button left of the slots. Settings has switches for Time and stars, Sound, and Vibration.

I'm stuck. Tap the ? button right of the slots: it points at a box to tap next, three times a round. Every level has at least one winning order. Use the ↻ button under the gear to start the round over.

How do I play an earlier picture? Tap the level counter at the top left to open the picker. Pictures unlock as you clear the one before them.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.
